# Utility OT Compliance & Operations Portfolio

This portfolio contains fictional, training-only artifacts designed to demonstrate documentation, tracking, risk-awareness, and process-coordination skills relevant to regulated utility and operational-technology (OT) environments.

No client, employer, proprietary, operational, or real production-system data is included. These materials do not represent direct NERC CIP implementation or compliance determinations.

---

## Demonstrated Capabilities

* **Compliance & Evidence Documentation:** Structuring controlled audit trails, asset registers, and verification evidence.
* **Operational Risk Tracking & Escalation:** Prioritizing critical infrastructure vulnerabilities and handling SLAs.
* **Vulnerability & Patch Coordination:** Managing patch workflows and compensating controls for OT assets.
* **Change-Management Process Support:** Documenting operational/security impact assessments, testing, and rollback plans.
* **Cross-Functional Communication:** Translating technical risks into operational escalation memos and audit summaries.
* **Audit-Readiness Organization:** Ensuring completeness, currency, traceability, and retrievability of operational artifacts.
